About Village Flood Alert

Building community safety through modern technology and preparedness

Our Mission

Village Flood Alert is a specialized community disaster management platform designed to protect our area through real-time monitoring and instant response. We aim to bridge the gap between hazard detection and emergency mobilization.

Key Features

Our platform integrates multiple critical systems into one cohesive safety network:

  • Real-Time Monitoring: Integration with weather and seismic APIs for instant hazard detection.
  • Interactive Mapping: Visualizing safe zones, high-risk areas, and evacuation routes on-the-fly.
  • AI Safety Assistant: Rule-based guidance for disaster protocols and first-aid instructions.
  • Location-Based SOS: One-click emergency alerts that share your coordinates with responders.

Why It Matters

During emergencies, every second counts. Village Flood Alert reduces response times and ensures that everyone in the community has access to the right information at the right time.
