First Aid & Preparedness

Comprehensive guides for handling standard disasters and medical emergencies

Earthquake Safety

Before

Safe Spots: Identify sturdy tables or interior walls.

Exits: Know two ways out of every room.

Secure Items: Bolt heavy furniture to walls.

Emergency Kit: Prepare food, water, and first aid.

During

DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON. Stay away from glass and heavy objects. Do not use elevators.

After

Expect aftershocks. Check for injuries and exit damaged buildings via stairs.

Flood Safety

Before

Identify high ground and prepare a waterproof emergency kit.

During

Move to higher ground immediately. Never drive or walk through floodwaters.

After

Wait for official clearance. Watch for contaminated water and electrical hazards.

Fire Safety

Before

Install smoke detectors and practice evacuation routes.

During

Alert others. Evacuate immediately via stairs. Crawl under smoke.

After

Call 101 from a safe location. Do not return inside until cleared.

Cyclone Safety

Before

Secure outdoor objects and board windows if possible.

During

Stay indoors in a small, windowless interior room.

After

Beware of fallen power lines and unstable structures.

First Aid Basics

Severe Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth. Elevate if possible.

Fractures

Immobilize the area. Apply ice packs if available (R.I.C.E).

CPR Basics

Ensure scene safety. Call 102. Push hard and fast in the center of the chest.
